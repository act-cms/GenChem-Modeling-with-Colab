Lesson 01: Introduction to Google Colab, Python, and Gemini
=======================

The purpose of this lesson is to introduce students to Google Colab, Gemini, and Python programming language.

## Lesson Information

This activity was piloted during a 75-minute recitation session in the first week of a second-semester general chemistry course. The session was led by an undergraduate teaching assistant (TA), with support from three undergraduate learning assistants (LAs) who assisted students and troubleshot technical issues. The TA facilitated the activity through live coding, beginning with a brief 5-minute introduction to basic Python syntax, variables, and functions.

Students were expected to use only the provided Google Colab notebook and Gemini for data processing and plotting tasks. All data interpretation and analysis were completed offline using an accompanying guided-inquiry worksheet (PDF format). At the end of the session, students submitted both their completed Colab notebook and guided-inquiry worksheet for participation credit.

### Lesson Learning Outcomes
At the end of this lesson, students will be able to...
1. Analyze how volume changes with temperature from a data table.
2. Construct a volume vs. temperature graph and interpret the meaning of the slope and intercepts.
3. Derive the mathematical relationship from the graph and data. Describe the relationship between volume and temperature and connect it to the Ideal Gas Law, PV = nRT.

### Cyberinfrastructure Prerequisites

Students will use Google Colab and Gemini. No prior programming experience is required. 

### Content Prerequisites

Before beginning this lesson, students are expected to be familiar with the
following content topics:

* Linear functions

* Ideal Gas Laws
